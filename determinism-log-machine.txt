  Every difference is exactly 1 ULP, e.g.
      B.ncdf[063]  0x1.aed68af0463c8p-2  ->  0x1.aed68af0463c7p-2
      B.erfc[077]  0x1.6f5a9c2697cc7p-4  ->  0x1.6f5a9c2697cc6p-4

  Sections A (exp/log/sqrt chains), C (all summation orderings incl. fsum
  and Kahan) and E (matrix reduction) are BIT-IDENTICAL across architectures.
  Divergence is confined entirely to the erf family.

  This is expected. glibc's erf is not correctly rounded and the riscv64 and
  amd64 implementations differ in the last place on 5 of 153 test points.
  It is not a defect, and it does not affect the machine-to-machine claim.
